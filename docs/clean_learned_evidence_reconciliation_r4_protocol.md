# Clean Learned Evidence Reconciliation R4 Protocol

## Status

**`R4_PROTOCOL_FROZEN_NOT_EXECUTED`**

Protocol date: 2026-09-07

Working method name: **Evidence Reconciliation Network (ERN)**.

This document prospectively freezes R4 before implementation or training. It
does not report an R4 result, authorize historical
`evidence_guidance_stage_e2_v1`, access external77, introduce noisy or real
evidence, or modify any frozen R2/R3 artifact, baseline, threshold, metric,
split, or evaluation universe.

## 1. Scientific hypothesis and scope

R4 tests whether a simple learned post-hoc model can combine sparse clean
delivered evidence with observable context from an immutable original RNA
secondary-structure prediction to identify residual false-positive pairs while
preserving pairs that the source predictor already predicted correctly.

The minimum hypothesis is:

> ERN can provide a better high-preservation FP-removal operating point than
> the frozen R3-P3 prediction-only comparator, and its advantage over a matched
> evidence-masked learned control can be attributed to usable evidence rather
> than capacity alone.

R4 is a clean symbolic-evidence development experiment on Legacy121. It does
not support noisy-evidence, real-evidence, independent-generalization,
model-agnostic, or 2D-to-3D claims.

## 2. Candidate unit, label, and edit space

For RNA sequence `x`, immutable source pair set `S`, delivered evidence set
`E`, and candidate `p=(i,j) in S`, ERN estimates:

```text
q_ij = P(DELETE/FP | x, S, E, permitted frozen inference-time signals)
```

- primary candidate unit: one original predicted pair under one frozen clean
  evidence realization;
- positive label: `DELETE/FP = 1`, meaning `p` is absent from the frozen GT;
- negative label: `KEEP/TP = 0`, meaning `p` is present in the frozen GT;
- GT is used for training labels and evaluation only;
- missing GT pairs are not candidates;
- R4 may only keep or delete an original pair.

Adding a pair, injecting a positive-evidence pair, reassigning a partner,
rebuilding a stem, or using a global pair decoder is prohibited in primary R4.

## 3. Frozen data and split universe

R4 uses only the existing Legacy121 development assets and the exact R2 v1.0.2
Track E matched universe:

- 121 RNAs and 363 source records: RNAfold, PETfold, and trRosettaRNA2;
- 3,523 eligible positive-pair evidence manifests;
- 3,630 eligible unpaired-nucleotide evidence manifests;
- 7,153 total eligible manifests;
- 310,838 original-pair realization events in the complete R3 Track E table.

The 87 crossing-ineligible and 20 minimum-loop-ineligible pair manifests remain
outside R4. No evidence is regenerated, resampled, weakened, or rewritten.
The exact manifest IDs and payload hashes are inherited from R2/R3.

The frozen grouped split is
`results/selective_refiner_protocol/legacy121_grouped_cv_folds.csv`, SHA256
`810b04a3963acc7637b60fcb5c2246c765fac334f809a5af9f8f050824ed974f`.
For rotation `k`:

```text
test       = fold k
validation = fold (k+1) mod 5
training   = the remaining three folds
```

RNA is the biological split unit. All source records, original pairs, evidence
channels, densities, evidence seeds, and manifestations belonging to one RNA
remain in the same role. Identity components may not cross folds. Repeated
pair-realizations are inference contexts, not biological replicates.

Training fits model parameters and preprocessing statistics. Validation may
select checkpoints, calibration parameters, and the operating threshold. Test
is held out from all such choices. external77 is absent from every input path.

## 4. Frozen feature allowlist

Every model feature must be available at inference time. No feature selection
or alternate feature set may be chosen after held-out R4 results are seen.

### 4.1 Original-prediction context

R4 legally reuses the historical source-agnostic 78-dimensional candidate
encoding implemented from the immutable original prediction:

- endpoint bases `base_i`, `base_j` and ordered `pair_type`;
- sequence length, raw separation, and relative separation;
- singleton flag;
- strict-stem length, stem-pair position, and normalized stem position;
- outer- and inner-boundary flags;
- immediate outward/inward stacked-neighbor existence and ordered pair type;
- the exact historical categorical vocabularies and unknown buckets, including
  the retained inward-neighbor `GG` unknown-bucket behavior.

The following frozen R3 signals are added:

- P2 exact cross-model support, encoded as `support_other_count / 2`;
- P4 sequence-only RNAfold base-pair probability `P_RNAfold(i,j)` generated by
  the frozen RNAfold 2.4.17 interface.

These two numeric values extend the candidate input from 78 to 80 dimensions.
Their preprocessing statistics are fit on training RNAs only.

### 4.2 Evidence-derived features

For each candidate and delivered item, R4 reuses the historical E2
candidate-relative geometry definitions:

- positive-pair item: the frozen 17-dimensional endpoint-distance, span,
  midpoint, exact-match/shared-endpoint/direct-conflict, and
  `CONTAINING/NESTED/CROSSING/DISJOINT` relation vector;
- unpaired-nucleotide item: the frozen 8-dimensional endpoint-distance,
  midpoint-distance, endpoint-equality, and strict-interval-membership vector;
- coordinate denominator: `max(sequence_length-1,1)`;
- evidence-set descriptors: delivered item count and count divided by sequence
  length;
- explicit E1 `LOCAL_CONFLICT` indicator for the candidate;
- explicit positive-pair exact-support indicator, zero for the unpaired
  channel.

Only delivered items are visible. Nominal density, evidence seed, full evidence
universe size, manifest GT hash, original clean item behind a corrupted item,
and unselected evidence-universe members are metadata, not features.

### 4.3 Frozen R3 signals excluded from primary model input

- P0 is excluded because it is a fold-level prevalence reference, not a
  candidate-specific inference signal.
- P1 and P3 are excluded because they contain historical learned-score
  provenance and P3 must remain an independent frozen comparator.
- E2 B2 disagreement is excluded so primary ERN does not require B2 refolding
  as an inference feature; B2/E2 remain independent references.

Adding any excluded signal to the primary model requires a prospective protocol
amendment made before R4 held-out results. It cannot rescue a failed R4.

### 4.4 Forbidden inputs

Forbidden model inputs include GT labels at inference, complete GT structure
beyond explicitly delivered clean evidence, unselected GT pairs or unpaired
positions, TP/FP/FN or wrong-partner annotations, source-specific error
summaries, predictor identity, family or dataset identity, R2/R3 held-out
outcomes, external77, noisy evidence, and real-modality data.

Predictor identity is retained only as evaluation metadata. It is not an ERN
feature or an allowed shortcut.

## 5. Evidence representation and ERN architecture

Positive-pair and unpaired-nucleotide channels are prespecified separate model
families because their item semantics and vector dimensions differ. They use
the same candidate branch, fusion depth, optimization, seeds, and evaluation
contract. A candidate realization belongs to exactly one evidence channel; the
two item types are never mixed inside one set encoder.

For each channel, the simple DeepSets-style ERN is:

```text
candidate encoder:
    Linear(80,64) -> ReLU

shared evidence-item encoder:
    Linear(item_dim,32) -> ReLU -> Linear(32,32) -> ReLU

permutation-invariant aggregation:
    masked elementwise mean || masked elementwise maximum  # 64 dimensions

evidence descriptors:
    item_count, item_count/L, E1_local_conflict, direct_pair_support  # 4

fusion classifier:
    Linear(132,128) -> ReLU -> Dropout(0.10)
    -> Linear(128,64) -> ReLU -> Dropout(0.10)
    -> Linear(64,1)
```

`item_dim` is 17 for positive-pair evidence and 8 for unpaired evidence. The
output is one DELETE logit and its sigmoid probability. Padding is excluded
from mean/max pooling. A genuinely empty or masked evidence set produces an
exact all-zero 68-dimensional evidence block after preprocessing.

No attention, Transformer, GNN, recurrence, pretrained language model,
foundation model, source embedding, RNA embedding, or global structure decoder
is allowed in the first R4 execution.

## 6. B4 matched evidence-masked learned control

B4 is trained separately but paired exactly with ERN by channel, fold, and
model seed. It uses:

- the same candidate rows and labels;
- the same train/validation/test RNA assignments;
- the same 80-dimensional candidate features;
- the same architecture, nominal capacity, initialization seed, optimizer,
  batch order, maximum epochs, early-stopping rule, and calibration/threshold
  procedures;
- an exact all-zero evidence block for every training, validation, and test
  row.

The only experimental difference is availability of usable evidence. B4 may
not receive item count, evidence channel, E1 conflict, direct support, nominal
density, or any other proxy for the masked set. B4 is not calibrated or
thresholded using ERN test outcomes. The paired `ERN - B4` comparison is the
primary evidence-attribution analysis.

## 7. Training and checkpoint selection

The next task may implement and execute, but this protocol task does not.
Frozen settings for each condition x channel x fold x seed are:

- model seeds: `17, 29, 41, 53, 67`;
- loss: class-weighted `BCEWithLogitsLoss`, DELETE positive;
- `pos_weight = KEEP_train / DELETE_train`, from training rows only;
- optimizer: AdamW, learning rate `1e-3`, weight decay `1e-4`;
- batch size: 256 pair-realization rows;
- maximum epochs: 100; early-stopping patience: 12;
- gradient-norm clipping: 5.0;
- no label-aware sampling and no density-specific model;
- checkpoint selection: lowest validation unweighted binary log loss;
- checkpoint tie-break: higher validation AUPRC, then earlier epoch.

Preprocessing, class weights, and all learned parameters use training data
only. Checkpoint selection uses validation only. Test results cannot change an
epoch, seed, hyperparameter, architecture, or feature.

## 8. Calibration

The primary output is a calibrated DELETE/pair-error probability. Per
condition x channel x fold x seed, fit one monotone Platt calibrator
`sigmoid(a*logit+b)` with `a >= 0` by minimizing unweighted binary log loss on
validation rows only. Both classes must be present and optimization must
converge; otherwise the run fails closed.

The calibrator is applied unchanged to that fold's held-out rows. ERN and B4
receive separately fitted calibrators under the same algorithm. No test label,
source-specific test subset, density-specific test subset, or external data may
fit or select calibration. Report event-pooled and RNA-balanced Brier score,
the frozen R3 ten-bin ECE, and reliability diagrams.

## 9. Validation-only operating point

For each condition x channel x fold x seed, candidate thresholds are all unique
calibrated validation probabilities plus delete-none. Equal scores enter as a
block. A threshold is eligible only if validation:

```text
event-pooled TP_preservation >= 0.99
RNA-balanced TP_preservation >= 0.99
```

Among eligible thresholds:

1. maximize RNA-balanced FP removal;
2. then maximize RNA-balanced modification precision;
3. then choose fewer deleted pairs;
4. then choose the numerically higher threshold.

The selected threshold is locked and applied unchanged to the held-out fold.
Density- or source-specific thresholds are prohibited. Held-out labels may not
move or rescue a threshold. No model seed is selected after results; all five
are reported and summarized by arithmetic mean, population standard deviation,
minimum, and maximum.

## 10. KEEP, DELETE, and ABSTAIN

When a valid threshold exists:

- `DELETE`: calibrated risk is greater than or equal to the locked threshold;
- `KEEP`: calibrated risk is below the locked threshold;
- the output remains a subset of the immutable original pair set.

`ABSTAIN` is a fail-closed state for a run lacking a valid calibration,
required input, checkpoint, or validation threshold. Every candidate in that
run remains unchanged and is recorded as `ABSTAIN_NO_REFINEMENT`. Abstention
does not count as a successful threshold and causes Gate B failure; it cannot
be invoked after viewing a difficult test RNA or used to remove failed samples.

## 11. Required comparisons

Every formal R4 report must include, on its exact applicable matched universe:

- B0 Original predictor;
- B1 local hard evidence;
- B2 global evidence-constrained refolding (`FULL_REFOLD_REFERENCE`);
- R3-P3 `V3_VETO2_FIXED`;
- R3-E1 `LOCAL_CONFLICT`;
- B4 matched evidence-masked learned control;
- learned R4 ERN.

P3 and E1 retain their frozen decisions and values. B2 retains its frozen
v1.0.2 structures and action-space warning. No comparator is retrained,
retuned, recalibrated, or rethresholded using R4 held-out results.

## 12. Metrics and aggregation

### Reliability

- non-interpolated AUPRC for DELETE/FP, primary;
- tie-aware AUROC, secondary;
- Brier score;
- frozen ten-bin event-pooled and RNA-balanced ECE;
- reliability diagrams and positive prevalence.

### Selective-correction utility

- TP preservation;
- FP removal;
- modification precision;
- coverage;
- delta F1;
- Precision, Recall, and F1 after deletion;
- complete beneficial/harmful deletion and TP/FP/FN accounting.

### Scope decomposition

Use the frozen delivered-evidence-defined exhaustive partition:

- `DIRECT`;
- `LOCAL_CONFLICT`;
- `NON_EVIDENCED`.

Report each scope's opportunities, deletions, removed FP, lost TP,
modification precision, preservation, coverage, and delta F1. ERN and B4 use
the same evidence-defined scopes even though B4 does not receive evidence.

### Evidence efficiency

For every nonzero-evidence stratum, report:

- `FP_removed / evidence_items`;
- `delta_F1 / evidence_items`.

Zero-evidence denominators remain NA.

### Aggregation and source-wise reporting

Event-pooled metrics pool inference events. RNA-balanced metrics first
aggregate all applicable repeated contexts within RNA and then weight each RNA
equally. Evidence/model seeds are not biological replicates. Formal source-wise
tables report RNAfold, PETfold, and trRosettaRNA2 at the same locked policies.

The primary combined Track E summary concatenates held-out predictions from
the separately frozen positive-pair and unpaired model families over all 7,153
eligible manifests before applying the aggregation definitions. It is not a
third jointly trained model, an ensemble, or an opportunity to select the
better channel. Channel-specific summaries remain mandatory companions.

## 13. Frozen Gate B

Gate B is conjunctive. Learned R4 supplies evidence beyond the frozen R3
baseline only if its held-out, five-seed mean primary combined Track E summary
defined above satisfies all of:

```text
event-pooled TP_preservation       >= 0.99
RNA-balanced TP_preservation       >= 0.99
RNA-balanced FP_removal            >  0.489748
event-pooled FP_removal            >  0.347816
```

It must also:

- compare with E1 at RNA-balanced FP removal `0.142946` and TP preservation
  `1.0`;
- show positive RNA-balanced FP-removal improvement over P3 in at least two of
  the three predictor sources, including at least one of RNAfold or PETfold;
- use one pooled policy without source-specific held-out thresholds;
- complete every required channel/fold/seed run without rescue selection.

B4 is a mandatory evidence-attribution control, not an additional numerical
Gate B bar. ERN-versus-B4 reliability and utility differences must be reported
at their separately validation-selected, dual-safe operating points. If ERN
does not improve on matched B4, Gate B is still evaluated exactly by the frozen
criteria above, but the specific claim that improvement is caused by usable
evidence is unsupported.

Strict `>` is required for FP-removal bars. Equality is not a pass. If any
condition fails, status is `R4_GATE_B_FAIL`. Failure does not authorize an
automatic Transformer, GNN, foundation model, larger hyperparameter search,
source-aware shortcut, feature addition, threshold rescue, or Legacy121
retuning.

## 14. B2 and Gate A

B2 is a `FULL_REFOLD_REFERENCE`, whereas R4 deletes only original predicted
pairs. B2 may add and replace pairs, so aggregate delta F1 is not an
action-matched primary endpoint. The formal comparison must jointly report TP
preservation, FP removal, modification precision, full edit accounting, and
the correction-preservation plane.

R4 may remove less FP than B2 while offering a safer point; neither outcome is
declared superior from one metric alone. Gate A remains:

**`GATE_A_DEFERRED_R4_REQUIRED`**

Gate A is decided only after completed R4 results are compared with frozen B2.
